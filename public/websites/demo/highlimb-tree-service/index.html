About

About Highlimb

Tree work for homes in Cedar Hollow and the towns around it.

Every job starts with a walk around the tree and a clear price before any cutting begins. You know what is coming down, what stays and what it costs.

Big trees near houses come down in sections, limbs get lowered by rope instead of dropped, and the yard gets raked clean before the truck leaves.

Good trimming keeps a tree strong for years, and good removal keeps everything around it in one piece. Both take patience, and that is how the work gets done.

Questions

Questions we hear a lot

When is the best time to trim?

Late fall through winter for most trees, while they are dormant. Dead or dangerous limbs can come off any time of year.

Is cleanup included?

Yes. Brush is chipped and hauled away and the yard is raked. If you want the firewood, it can be cut to length and left for you.

Can you grind the stump too?

Yes. The stump can be ground on the same visit or on a later day, whichever works better for you.

A tree came down in a storm. Can you come now?

Call. A tree on a house, a car or a driveway goes to the top of the list.

Got a tree to deal with?

Text a photo and the address, or just call.
