Tree Removal

Dead, leaning or unwanted trees taken down and hauled off.

Tree removal in Cedar Hollow for trees that are dead, dying, leaning toward the house or just in the way. Big trees close to homes and fences come down in sections so nothing gets hit on the way down. Once the tree is on the ground, the wood and brush get cut up and hauled away, and the yard is raked clean. If you want to keep the firewood, just say so. Every job starts with a look at the tree and a clear price before any cutting begins.

What's included

  • Dead and dying trees

    Trees that are dropping limbs or have stopped leafing out.

  • Leaning trees

    Trees leaning toward a house, shed, fence or driveway.

  • Tight spaces

    Trees close to buildings taken down in sections.

  • Haul away

    Wood, limbs and brush cut up and removed.

  • Firewood on request

    Keep the wood, cut to length, if you want it.

  • Yard cleanup

    Raked and cleaned up before we leave.
