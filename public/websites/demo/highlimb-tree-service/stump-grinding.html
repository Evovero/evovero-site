Stump Grinding

Old stumps ground down below the grass line.

Stump grinding in Cedar Hollow for old stumps that trip people, catch the mower and keep sprouting. The stump gets ground down below the grass line so the spot can be filled and seeded or planted over. Surface roots near the stump can be ground out at the same time. Grinding works on fresh stumps from a tree that just came down and on old ones that have sat in the yard for years. The chips can be left to fill the hole or hauled away.

What's included

  • Single stumps

    One stump or a few around the yard.

  • Below grade grinding

    Ground down below the grass line.

  • Surface roots

    Roots near the stump ground out too.

  • Fresh or old stumps

    New cuts and stumps that have sat for years.

  • Chip cleanup

    Chips left to fill the hole or hauled away.
